Glow Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Glow Pop ("the app") is a casual swipe-to-pop game published on Google Play by Rahul Kumar Saha ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, best scores, tutorial status, free-gift timer and your music, sound and vibration settings) is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. We do not operate servers, do not require an account or sign-in, and do not receive or store this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The game can be played without watching ads. If you choose to watch an optional rewarded video (to continue after losing, skip a hard level, or claim a free gift), the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). All ad requests are marked as child-directed and limited to general-audience (G-rated) content, so ads are not personalised, and the app does not request the advertising ID permission. To request, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device or app identifiers (such as the app set ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, app performance and diagnostic information

This data is collected directly by Google, transmitted encrypted, and handled under Google's policies. We do not sell personal information.

Consent and privacy choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states). When privacy options are required, an AD PRIVACY button on the main menu lets you review or change your choices at any time.

Purchases

The app does not sell anything and contains no in-app purchases.

Children

Glow Pop is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself does not collect personal information from anyone.
