ExpenseTrack

Track your daily and monthly expenses automatically from bank SMS. Your SMS and financial data stay on your phone.

Highlights

  • Reads bank SMS alerts on your device to record card and UPI expenses
  • Spending calendar, daily and monthly charts, per bank and card
  • No account, no cloud upload; SMS and financial data never leave your phone
  • Free with non-personalized ads; optional one-time purchase to remove ads
