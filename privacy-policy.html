ExpenseTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

ExpenseTrack ("the app") is an Android app developed by Rahul Kumar Saha that helps you track your expenses from bank SMS alerts. This policy explains what the app accesses, what stays on your phone, and what is shared with third parties.

Summary

  • Your SMS messages and financial data never leave your phone. They are not uploaded, sold, or shared with anyone, including advertisers.
  • The app shows non-personalized ads from Google AdMob. The ad service receives standard device and network information needed to deliver ads, but never your SMS or transactions.
  • You can remove ads with a one-time purchase handled by Google Play.
  • There is no account, sign-in, analytics, or cloud backup.

Information the app accesses

SMS messages (on your phone only)

With your permission (Read SMS and Receive SMS), the app reads existing and incoming SMS to identify expense alerts from banks and card issuers. It records the amount, date, merchant or payee, bank, and the last four digits of the account or card, together with the original alert text, so that you can review it.

  • Messages that are not financial alerts are skipped and not stored.
  • Processing happens entirely on your device. No SMS content is sent over the internet.
  • The app never sends SMS and does not read WhatsApp or other messaging apps.

Your decisions

Choices you make (include, exclude, remembered payee choices) are stored on your device.

What stays on your device

All parsed transactions, original alert text, review decisions, remembered choices, and scan progress are stored in the app's private storage. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays a small number of clearly labelled native ads (at the end of Home and between days in Activity). Ads are requested as non-personalized and the app does not request the device advertising ID.

To deliver ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect information such as your IP address, device type and model, operating system version, app information, approximate location derived from IP, and interactions with ads. This collection is performed by Google under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app never provides SMS content, transaction amounts, merchants, payees, bank or card details, or any other financial information to AdMob or any other party.

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads are requested. You can change that choice later in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy options when it is shown.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The app receives only a confirmation that the purchase was made; it does not receive your payment details. The purchase status is cached on your device so ads stay off.

Permissions

Permission Why it is used
Read SMS, Receive SMS Find and record expense alerts on your device
Internet, network state Load ads and check the Remove-ads purchase with Google Play
Billing Offer the one-time Remove-ads purchase

The app does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, or the advertising ID.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you delete it. To delete all app data, open Android Settings → Apps → ExpenseTrack → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Revoking SMS permission stops future reading but keeps existing history until you delete it. Because the developer does not receive your data, there is nothing stored on our side to delete.

Children

ExpenseTrack is a personal-finance app intended for adults and is not directed to children. Ads are limited to a general (PG) content rating.

Security

Data is kept in app-private storage protected by Android's application sandbox. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone may be able to view the app; use a screen lock.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date, and significant changes will be highlighted in the app or store listing.
