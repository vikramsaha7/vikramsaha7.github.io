Skyfront Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Skyfront ("the app") is a flight-combat game published on Google Play by Rahul Kumar Saha ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (mission stars, selected aircraft, number of sorties and your sound preference) is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. We do not operate servers, do not require an account or sign-in, and do not receive or store this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The campaign can be played offline without ads. If you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad to revive after losing a mission, the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To request, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions, app performance and diagnostic information

This data is collected directly by Google, transmitted encrypted, and handled under Google's policies. We do not sell personal information. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings.

Consent and privacy choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states). When privacy options are required, an AD PRIVACY button in the app lets you review or change your choices at any time.

Purchases

The app does not currently sell anything. If an optional cosmetic purchase is offered in the future, payments will be processed by Google Play; we will not receive your payment card details, and this policy will be updated.

Children

Skyfront is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
